The figures and their original captions were extracted as PNG images from the paper PDF supplied by an author. No values were estimated, no data was reconstructed, and no colors were changed. These are published results, not new measurements from this repository.

Extraction: PDF pages 5 and 8 were rendered with `pdftoppm` at a longest-side resolution of 3000 px, then cropped with Pillow. The crop coordinates (left, top, right, bottom) are `(1194, 261, 2070, 639)` and `(234, 261, 2070, 1086)`, respectively.
